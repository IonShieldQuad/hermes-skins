# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public build, ported from the mainframe-access design kit (contract v2).

- One plate per tool call, file card and diff; prose stays unframed; thinking rows stay bare.
- Prompt and composer: black face, full-accent hairline, wider bloom than the plates.
- Composer aligned to the transcript column with `translate: none` and 24 px insets.
- Sidebar seam glow painted as a pseudo-element strip over the thread, 72 px falloff.
- Selected chips (clarify letters, primary buttons) get the ground colour as ink: the app
  pairs its pale `ui_primary` with white text, which is unreadable at 13 px.
- 13 px body base, Chakra Petch plus JetBrains Mono, square corners throughout.
